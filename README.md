# Black Rose West Allis - Website Demo

A live WordPress demo of a proposed website for Black Rose, the cocktail bar with elevated Italian street food at Makers Row, 6601 W. National Ave., West Allis.

**Open the demo:** https://playground.wordpress.net/?blueprint-url=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shotgunchris77/black-rose-demo/main/black-rose-demo.zip

The demo runs in WordPress Playground in your browser. Nothing is saved between visits, and it takes about 20-30 seconds to load.

## What's inside
- Home, Menu (Cocktails, N/A Drinks, Beer, Food drop-downs), Events calendar and Visit pages
- An Events post type with a monthly calendar (Dashboard > Events)
- Links to [@blackrosewestallis](https://www.instagram.com/blackrosewestallis/)

## Notes
- Menu items and events are samples. Photos are Unsplash placeholders plus the logo and images from Black Rose's Instagram.
- `Black Rose Demo Package.zip` holds `blueprint.json` and `theme.zip`. Editable source is in `source/`.
