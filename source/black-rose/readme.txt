Block theme for Black Rose West Allis, a cocktail bar with elevated Italian street food.

== Editing ==
* Events: Dashboard > Events > Add New. Fill in the date, start/end time and an optional tag in the "Event date & time" box. Events show on the Events calendar and the homepage automatically.
* Menu: Pages > Menu. Each section (Cocktails, N/A Drinks, Beer, Food) is a Details block. Edit the item names and descriptions in place.
* Scrolling tape: Appearance > Editor > Patterns > Template Parts > Tape Banner.
* Hours and address: footer template part, the Visit page and the Find Us section on the Home page.
